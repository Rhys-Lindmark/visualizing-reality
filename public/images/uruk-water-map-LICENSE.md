# Uruk water-map image attribution

`mesopotamia-palaeochannels.webp` adapts Figure 1 and
`eridu-irrigation-network.webp` adapts Figure 5 from:

Jaafar Jotheri et al., “Identifying the preserved network of irrigation canals
in the Eridu region, southern Mesopotamia,” *Antiquity* 99 (2025),
https://doi.org/10.15184/aqy.2025.19.

The article and figures are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution
4.0 International license: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.

The web assets preserve the original figure content and were changed only by
lossy WebP compression and resizing. The map layers and legends remain the
authors’ own. The site’s labels outside the images emphasize that Figure 1’s
palaeochannels are a mixed-age palimpsest and that Figure 5’s network combines
features from many occupations rather than one simultaneous system.
