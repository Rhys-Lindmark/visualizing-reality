# Iron adoption: evidence audit

## Claim

Iron did not replace bronze at discovery. Across five regional windows, first attestation and sustained adoption are different clocks. Ore, charcoal, furnace control, bloom consolidation or casting, smithing, exchange, and institutions had to become reproducible before iron became ordinary.

## Process boundary

The production chain follows Devereaux's accessible technical synthesis: ore must be recognized and prepared; wood must become charcoal; a furnace and air supply reduce rather than melt iron in the bloomery route; the slag-rich bloom must be repeatedly consolidated; and the resulting iron must be forged and, where practiced, carburized or heat-treated. Qian and Hung document a distinct early Chinese cast-iron pathway connected to bronze casting, ceramics, and political context.

The timeline bars are editorial synthesis windows, not annual series, confidence intervals, or measured diffusion rates. The page calculates no hardness, superiority, adoption, fuel, forest-loss, output, military-power, or civilizational score. Bronze persistence is not treated as failure to innovate.
