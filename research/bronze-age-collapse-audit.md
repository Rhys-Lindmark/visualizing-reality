# Late Bronze Age transformations: evidence audit

## Claim

The Late Bronze Age did not end in one year. Palaces, capitals, archives, ports, and states changed on different dates, and some political systems survived. The comparison therefore tests a systems-failure interpretation without promoting drought, invasion, earthquake, rebellion, or trade contraction into a universal cause.

## Evidence windows

| Window | What is retained | Sources | What is blocked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Eastern Mediterranean | Knapp and Manning's comparative conclusion that transformations were non-synchronous and explanations remain plural | `KNAPP_MANNING2016` | One collapse year, one causal score, or one basin-wide population-loss estimate |
| Pylos | A burned palace and roughly one thousand Linear B texts, mostly from its final administrative horizon | `JUDSON2023` | An identified attacker, a Sea Peoples attribution, or an uncontested exact year |
| Hattusa | Central Anatolian tree-ring/isotope drought signal in 1198–1196 BCE; emptied capital later burned; central administration ceased | `MANNING_ET_AL2023` | Drought as a sufficient cause, burning as the moment of evacuation, or local harvest totals |
| Ugarit and Gibala | Threat correspondence and a proposed 1192–1190 BCE northern Levant destruction window | `KANIEWSKI_ET_AL2011`; `HALAYQA2010`; `KNAPP_MANNING2016` | One homogeneous invading people or an exact date for the entire regional transition |
| Hala Sultan Tekke | Multi-phase destruction, rebuilding, production, exchange, and final abandonment around 1150 BCE | `FISCHER2023`; `OEA_HALA_SULTAN`; `KNAPP_MANNING2016` | Annual decline, a proven attacker, population death, or ethnic replacement |
| Egypt | Ramesses III's Year 8 royal representation of land and naval victories; Egyptian state survival | `MEDINET_HABU_OIP`; `KNAPP_MANNING2016` | A neutral campaign map, casualty series, common ethnicity, or universal causal attribution |

## Model boundary

The horizontal axis is visual chronology. Bands preserve source-specific ranges and are not uncertainty distributions. The four descriptive columns—environment, conflict, institution, and persistence—contain prose evidence, not additive variables. The dataset has no `collapse_score`, `cause_score`, `resilience_score`, `population_loss`, `annual_flow`, or `common_attacker` field.

The regional row is explicitly comparative and editorial. The other five rows are unlike local or textual windows. They cannot be ranked by severity, added into a total, or interpreted as a representative sample of every Late Bronze Age society.
